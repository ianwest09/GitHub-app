# Verified price research — Port Alfred, 19 November 2026

Prices for the *Christmas Lunch for Ten* shopping list, looked up at Pick n Pay, Woolworths and Spar. Every figure below is transcribed from a live source, with the URL and validity dates. **Nothing is estimated, invented or interpolated.**

## Read this first

1. **These are not Christmas Day prices.** Every current catalogue expires on **22 November 2026** — over a month before the lunch. Re-check or re-enter prices from till slips when you actually shop. The app must auto-expire these records.
2. **Coverage is partial.** Only a handful of the ~35 basket lines have any verified price, and only **two** lines have prices at more than one shop. A complete "cheapest basket" total **cannot** be computed from public data — the app must say "insufficient data" instead of filling gaps.
3. **Catalogue PDF text extraction garbles cents** (e.g. `79¹⁹¹` = R79.99, `49£99` = R49,99). Figures below are read as `Rxx.99`; confirm against the catalogue image before buying.
4. **Store vs online may differ.** Pick n Pay's catalogue says "Selected products may not be available at all stores" and is "Only available at Pick n Pay Supermarkets and Hypermarkets in the Eastern Cape" [2](https://cdnm.heyzine.com/flip-book/pdf/2e4baf279fe06cfb532f5802ec2764b23103d818.pdf). Woolworths states "Promotions in store and online may vary" [9](https://www.woolworths.co.za/browse/food).
5. **Loyalty conditions apply.** Several Pick n Pay prices are "Smart Shopper price" only, and several are multi-buy bundles. These are conditions, not straight unit prices.
6. **Host A is one of the two hosts in their 80s.** Use “Host A” for the stage 4 breast cancer dietary brief; boneless-chicken and dietary notes for the hosts include Host A.
7. **Held menu lines are unpriced.** Smoked gammon, the supplied ice-cream bombe base/mix-ins and the optional coconut rice pudding ingredients have no verified price entries; do not add inferred prices until preparation details, crowd batch count, mix-in quantities, source, labels and any required Host A/dietitian clearance are supplied.

## How the app should use this file

- Import these rows as `PriceEntry` records with `source = CATALOGUE` or `STORE_LISTING`, `store`, `branch = Port Alfred`, `validFrom`, `validTo`, `packSize`, `unit`, `isPromotion`, `loyaltyRequired`, `bundleCondition`, `sourceUrl` and `capturedAt = 2026-11-19`.
- **Hard-expire** every catalogue row on 23 Nov 2026; show "Expired — re-check price" rather than using it.
- Never auto-fill a missing shop, never substitute a non-like-for-like product, never convert a bundle into a plain unit price without surfacing the bundle condition.
- Where ≥2 shops have a current, like-for-like verified price, select the lowest normalised unit price and show the saving. Where they do not, show "Insufficient data to compare" and a one-tap "add price from till slip".
- Because Spar Port Alfred is closed on Christmas Day [4](https://www.guzzle.co.za/spar/port-alfred), flag any shopping task scheduled for 25 December and move it to 24 December or earlier.
