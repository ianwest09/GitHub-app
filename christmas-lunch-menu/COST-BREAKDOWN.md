- **Partial coverage.** Only ~5 of ~33 priced lines have a current verified price (Tier A). There is **no defensible full verified-basket total** — the ~R144/head figure is a planning estimate, clearly tiered.
- **Catalogue prices expire fast.** PnP Fresh Food People runs 23–29 Nov; Spar 23 Nov–6 Dec; Black Friday deals 26–29 Nov only. Re-check or re-enter at the till. Spar Port Alfred is closed 25 Dec.
