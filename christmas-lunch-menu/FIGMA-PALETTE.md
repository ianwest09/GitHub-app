## Color theory

- **Warm brown (30°)** comes directly from the image — it's the dominant mood.
- **Deep blue (210°)** is the direct complement (180° opposite) — maximum contrast, makes the warm tones pop.
- **Teal (180°)** is a split-complementary — cooler than brown but warmer than blue; bridges the two.
- **Gold (45°)** is analogous to brown — adds festive warmth without clashing.
- **Warm neutrals** carry a faint brown tint so they don't feel sterile next to the warm image.
