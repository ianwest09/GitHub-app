## Amber — options pending Host A + dietitian clearance

These are the **options** to discuss. None of them may be bought, prepared, served or priced until Host A and her oncology dietitian clear the final ingredient list and every packaged label. They are presented as choices, not as settled dishes.

### Option 1 — Homemade ice-cream bombe (recipe supplied)

- **Status:** Recipe supplied, **dietary clearance pending.**
- **Linked base:** [Allrecipes 2-Ingredient Ice Cream](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/256234/2-ingredient-ice-cream/) — `1 pint heavy whipping cream` + `1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk`, soft peaks, beat together, freeze ~6 hours, serves 4.
- **User bombe method:** line a medium round glass/stainless-steel bowl with two overlapping cling-wrap layers; fold in fruit mince + chopped pecans + glacé cherries; pack firmly, smooth, cover, freeze overnight.
- **Why it is held:** cream and condensed milk are **dairy and contain sugar**; glacé cherries contain sugar; fruit mince may contain **sugar, alcohol, suet/other animal fat** or other restricted ingredients. These conflict directly with Host A's no-dairy, no-sugar brief and the alcohol-free table.
- **What Host A's clearance would unlock (if granted):** a crowd batch count, mix-in quantities, a price, and a place on the dessert course.

### Option 2 — Coconut rice pudding (optional candidate)

- **Status:** Optional candidate, **Host A clearance pending.** Review only.
- **Previous ingredient list (for review):** rice simmered in coconut milk with cinnamon and a vanilla **pod** (not extract), layered with berries and toasted coconut; described as soft, with no added sugar, dairy, wheat or gelatine.
- **Why it is held:** the previous description is a **review item, not a safety claim.** Coconut milk is a packaged product whose label must be checked; berries, coconut and vanilla pod labels must be checked. Clearance is not automatic.
- **What Host A's clearance would unlock (if granted):** a confirmed ingredient list, quantities, a price, and a labelled dish and spoon on the table.

### Option 3 — Keep pears as the only dessert

- **Status:** Already shared-safe.
- The simplest, lowest-risk path: cinnamon-baked pears remain the sole shared dessert, and both amber options stay off the table. Nothing further to clear.

## Questions to confirm with Host A and her oncology dietitian

1. **Dessert path** — Option 1 (ice-cream bombe), Option 2 (coconut rice pudding), Option 3 (pears only), or a combination? Any clearance is for the **final ingredient list and every packaged label**, not for the recipe as written.
2. **Ice-cream bombe labels** — if pursued, can we accept a dairy + sugar dessert on Host A's plate at all, or is it only ever for the other nine? (The menu currently holds it off *everyone's* plate until cleared.)
3. **Fruit mince** — if the bombe is pursued, is there a brand whose label is free of alcohol, suet/other animal fat and added sugar that the dietitian will approve? If not, the bombe cannot proceed.
4. **Coconut rice pudding** — is coconut milk acceptable? Is a vanilla pod acceptable (vs. vanilla extract, which is excluded)?
5. **Smoked gammon** — confirm it stays off Host A's plate (it does); confirm whether it should appear at all, given it is cured/processed pork. Quantity/source/labels remain to be supplied separately.
6. **Medication-related diet** — discreetly, with both hosts (Host A included): warfarin/vitamin K, low salt, diabetes, or any chewing/swallowing/denture considerations.
7. **Alcohol-free table** — discreetly confirm the fully alcohol-free plan suits the guest in recovery (it is the safest default; nobody is singled out).
8. **Final label check** — every packaged item across the green dishes too (rice cakes, stock-free pantry items, canned beans, etc.).
