## On arrival — shared · alcohol-free

### 1. Lemon–herb white bean dip

**Ingredients (for 10):** 500 g cooked white beans (2 tins, drained & rinsed), 2 lemons (zest + juice), 2 garlic cloves, ~80 ml extra-virgin olive oil, small bunch flat-leaf parsley, sea salt, black pepper. Served with soft vegetable batons (cucumber, carrot, fennel), lettuce cups, and label-checked rice cakes / 100%-rice crackers.

**Verified cost:** No verified price for beans, lemons, garlic, olive oil, parsley or rice crackers — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Blanch the garlic briefly if a raw bite is unwanted; for both hosts, keep the flavour gentle.
2. Blitz the beans, garlic, lemon zest and juice, and most of the olive oil to a coarse-smooth dip. Add a little cold water if needed to loosen.
3. Fold through chopped parsley; season lightly with salt and pepper. Taste and adjust lemon.
4. Spoon into a bowl; finish with a drizzle of olive oil and a little more parsley.
5. Arrange batons and lettuce cups around it; keep rice crackers on the side, label-checked for no wheat, yeast or sugar.

### 2. Rosemary roasted almonds & pumpkin seeds (finely chopped)

**Ingredients (for 10):** 200 g almonds, 150 g pumpkin seeds, 1 sprig rosemary (needles removed), 1 tbsp olive oil, pinch of sea salt. *For both hosts: chop or grind finely after roasting.*

**Verified cost:** No verified price — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Heat oven to 180 °C. Toss almonds and pumpkin seeds with olive oil, chopped rosemary and a pinch of salt.
2. Spread on a tray and roast 8–10 min until fragrant, shaking once — do not let them char.
3. Cool, then **chop or grind finely** so both hosts can chew them easily. (Swap for soft avocado and cucumber cups if preferred.)

### 3. Drinks (one menu for everyone)

**Ingredients:** Rooibos tea bags, fresh mint, lemons, cucumber, sparkling water, granadilla & lime (for the sideboard spritzer only). Ice.

**Verified cost:** No verified price — pantry.

**Recipe:** Brew rooibos strong, cool, chill; serve over ice with mint and lemon. For cucumber–lemon water, add cucumber ribbons and lemon slices to a water jug. Keep Host A's jug unsweetened; the lightly sweetened granadilla & lime spritzer lives on the extras sideboard only.

## First course — shared

### 4. Chilled fennel, cucumber & avocado salad

**Ingredients (for 10):** 2 bulbs fennel, 3 cucumbers, 3 avocados, 1 bunch radish, 2 heads baby leaves, 1 small bunch dill or parsley, 100 g sunflower seeds (toasted, finely ground), 2 lemons (juice), extra-virgin olive oil, black pepper, sea salt.

**Verified cost:** No verified price — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Finely shred the fennel; slice the cucumber paper-thin; slice the radish thin; dice the avocado.
2. Toast sunflower seeds in a dry pan, then grind finely.
3. Dress the leaves with lemon juice and olive oil; season. Fold in fennel, cucumber, radish and avocado gently.
4. Finish with dill/parsley and ground sunflower seeds. Keep chilled until serving. **No vinegar, mustard or honey.**

## The main table — shared, all ten

### 5. Rosemary, lemon & garlic roast chicken

**Ingredients (for 10):** 10 boneless chicken thighs or supremes (~180 g each = ~1.8 kg), 2 lemons (1 juiced, 1 sliced), 6 garlic cloves (crushed), 3–4 sprigs rosemary (chopped), ~60 ml olive oil, sea salt, black pepper. No butter, no sugar glaze, no wine, no stock cubes.

**Verified cost:** **R143.98** — Pick n Pay Fresh Skinless Chicken Breast Fillets at R79.99/kg × 1.8 kg (verified, valid 16–22 Nov 2026). Woolworths equivalent ~R224.98 (R124.99/kg). Spar IQF is cheaper but frozen, bone-in mixed portions — not suitable. Re-check price at the till.

**Recipe:**
1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Combine olive oil, lemon juice, crushed garlic, rosemary, salt and pepper.
2. Toss the boneless chicken in the marinade; rest 20–30 min if time allows.
3. Arrange in a roasting tray (top shelf); tuck lemon slices and rosemary sprigs between pieces.
4. Roast ~35–40 min until cooked through and juices run clear — **no charred edges** (cover loosely with foil if browning too fast).
5. Rest 10 min before slicing. Reserve pan juices for the gravy.

### 6. Lemon & parsley pan gravy

**Ingredients:** Pan juices from the chicken, ~400 ml homemade chicken stock, 1 lemon (juice + zest), small bunch parsley (chopped), 1–2 tbsp cornflour (Maizena), cold water. No flour, yeast extract, wine or cream.

**Verified cost:** No verified price (cornflour is pantry) — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Drain the excess fat from the chicken roasting tray; keep the juices and browned bits.
2. Set the tray on the hob, add hot homemade stock and scrape up the bits.
3. Simmer and reduce by a third; add lemon juice and zest.
4. Slake cornflour with cold water; whisk in slowly until thickened to coating consistency.
5. Finish with chopped parsley; pour into a labelled jug.

### 7. Olive oil & rosemary roast baby potatoes

**Ingredients (for 10):** 1.5 kg baby potatoes, 3 sprigs rosemary, ~50 ml olive oil, sea salt, black pepper. No butter, no duck fat.

**Verified cost:** **R26.25** (Pick n Pay, conditional) — 1.5 kg at R17.50/kg, but only inside "BUY ANY 4 for R140" (so you must buy 4 qualifying items). **R29.99** at Woolworths (buy the 2 kg bag at R39.99 = R20/kg). No verified Spar price. Re-check at the till.

**Recipe:**
1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Halve or quarter the baby potatoes if large; parboil 8 min, drain, shake to roughen the edges.
2. Toss with olive oil, chopped rosemary, salt and pepper.
3. Roast on the lower shelf ~30–35 min, turning once, until crisp outside and soft inside.

### 8. Brown rice & lentil pilaf

**Ingredients (for 10):** 400 g brown rice, 200 g brown or red lentils, 1 onion (finely chopped), 2 garlic cloves, ~500 ml homemade vegetable stock, 2 spring onions, lemon zest, small bunch parsley, olive oil. (Quinoa 300 g + wild rice 200 g if available — not always stocked locally.)

**Verified cost:** No verified price — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Rinse rice and lentils. Sweat the onion and garlic in olive oil until soft (no browning).
2. Add rice and lentils; stir to coat. Pour in hot homemade stock (no cubes).
3. Cover and simmer until the liquid is absorbed and grains are tender (~30–35 min).
4. Fluff; fold through chopped spring onion, lemon zest and parsley. Reheat gently on the day.

### 9. Silky cauliflower purée

**Ingredients (for 10):** 2 large cauliflower heads, 4 garlic cloves, ~40 ml olive oil, pinch of nutmeg, sea salt. No cream, butter or cheese.

**Verified cost:** No verified price for cauliflower at PnP/Spar; Woolworths lists whole cauliflower ~R73.00/kg (avg 500 g head ≈ R35.04) — single-shop, no comparison. Enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Cut cauliflower into florets; simmer in lightly salted water (or steam) with the garlic until very tender.
2. Drain well; transfer to a blender with the olive oil and a pinch of nutmeg.
3. Blend until completely smooth and silky; season lightly. Reheat gently; do not let it catch.

### 10. Roasted green beans, baby carrots & courgettes

**Ingredients (for 10):** 400 g green beans, 400 g baby carrots, 400 g courgettes, 3 sprigs thyme, 1 lemon (zest), olive oil, sea salt, black pepper.

**Verified cost:** No verified price — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Heat oven to 200 °C. Trim beans; halve carrots lengthways if large; slice courgettes on the diagonal.
2. Toss all with olive oil, thyme, salt and pepper.
3. Roast ~20–25 min until tender (not al dente — soft for both hosts). Finish with lemon zest.

### 11. Mushroom, leek & celery "stuffing" bake

**Ingredients (for 10):** 400 g mushrooms, 3 leeks, 1 head celery, 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, ~150 g cooked brown rice (from the pilaf batch), small bunch parsley, olive oil, sea salt, black pepper. No bread, yeast, sausage, bacon or suet.

**Verified cost:** No verified price — enter from till slip. (PnP "Chopped Mixed Mushrooms 400 g BUY ANY 2 SAVE R10" gives no usable base price.)

**Recipe:**
1. Finely chop mushrooms, leeks, celery, onion and garlic.
2. Sweat in olive oil until soft and any mushroom liquid has evaporated.
3. Fold in cooked brown rice and chopped parsley; season.
4. Press into a baking dish; bake at 200 °C ~25 min until hot through with a light crust on top.

### 12. Fresh tomato, roast pepper & basil relish

**Ingredients (for 10):** 600 g tomatoes, 3 red peppers, 1 bunch basil, 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, olive oil, sea salt, black pepper. No sugar, no vinegar.

**Verified cost:** No verified price — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Roast the red peppers at 200 °C until skins blister; steam in a covered bowl, peel, and slice.
2. Blanch, peel and dice the tomatoes. Sweat onion and garlic in olive oil until soft.
3. Add tomatoes; simmer gently ~20 min until jammy but **unsweetened**. Fold in roasted peppers.
4. Tear in basil; season. Serve at room temperature as the sugar-free, vinegar-free stand-in for cranberry sauce.

## Separate platters — for some · own utensils

### 13. Slow-poached beef/ox tongue

**Ingredients (for 3):** 1 large beef/ox tongue (~1.5 kg), 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp peppercorns, 1 onion, 2 celery sticks, water. Served with parsley, lemon & olive oil salsa (below).

**Verified cost:** No verified price at any shop — must be ordered from the butcher; price from the till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Order from the butcher a week ahead. Place tongue in a deep pot with bay, peppercorns, onion and celery; cover with water.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer; poach about **4 hours** until tender (no wine, no vinegar brine).
3. Cool slightly; peel off the outer skin. Refrigerate in its own sealed container overnight.
4. On the day, slice **paper-thin** on its own board with its own knife; warm gently in its own pan. Own platter, own tongs — never touches the shared dishes or shared spoons.

## Dessert — shared-safe pears · options pending clearance

### 16. Cinnamon-baked pears with finely chopped toasted pecans *(shared-safe)*

**Ingredients (for 10):** 10 small pears, 1 tsp ground cinnamon, 150 g pecans (toasted, finely chopped), water or a little rooibos to moisten the dish. Sweetened only by the fruit itself — no sugar, honey or syrup.

**Verified cost:** No verified price — enter from till slip.

**Recipe:**
1. Heat oven to 180 °C. Halve and core the pears; leave stems on if you like.
2. Place cut-side up in a baking dish; dust with cinnamon; add a splash of water or rooibos to the dish.
3. Cover with foil; bake ~30–35 min until very soft. Uncover for the last 5–10 min if you want a little colour.
4. Toast pecans in a dry pan; **chop finely** for both hosts. Scatter over the pears before serving. **This remains the documented shared-safe dessert.**

### 17. Homemade ice-cream bombe *(recipe supplied, dietary clearance pending)*

**Ingredients (per the linked 4-serving base):** 1 pint (≈473 ml) heavy whipping cream, 1 (14 ounce / ≈397 g) can sweetened condensed milk, fruit mince + chopped pecans + glacé cherries (quantities pending). Crowd batch count and mix-in quantities **not supplied.**

**Verified cost:** No verified price — held until clearance; do not price yet.

**Recipe (as supplied — clearance pending):**
1. Beat the cream in a chilled glass or metal bowl until soft peaks.
2. Add the condensed milk; beat until thick and well combined.
3. Line a medium round glass or stainless-steel bowl neatly with **two overlapping layers of cling wrap**, leaving overhang.
4. Fold in the fruit mince + chopped pecans + glacé cherries for the classic speckled pudding look.
5. Pack firmly, smooth the top, cover tightly, and **freeze overnight**. (The linked base freezes ~6 hours in a shallow container; the bombe uses overnight for shape.)
