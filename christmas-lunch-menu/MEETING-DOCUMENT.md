## 6. Table setting — choose a theme

**Recommended layout:** Long rectangular table, 4 guests per side + 1 at each head. Table 2.4–3 m long for ~60 cm elbow room each.

### Theme 1: Coastal & Seaside Blues
Inspired by the Kowie River and Sunshine Coast. Crisp whites, ocean blues, natural woven textures — rattan chargers, driftwood, shells.

### Theme 2: South African Botanical & Fynbos
Indigenous greenery, succulents, proteas, earthy neutral linens. Holds up well in warm December weather.

### Theme 3: Outdoor Patio & Garden Long Table
Low-profile floral or citrus garlands down the centre so guests can pass platters and see across the table. Ideal for a shaded stoep or garden.

### Theme 4: Classic Festive Red & Crisp White
Traditional Christmas colours for summer: bright red napkins or berries with light white tablecloths and natural greenery. Airy, not heavy.

### Practical tips
- **Centrepiece height:** keep below 25 cm so sightlines stay clear for passing platters
- **Wind-proofing:** heavier ceramic dinnerware, weighted napkin rings, hurricane glass candle holders for the sea breeze
- **Shade:** serve in the shade; keep cold dishes in a cooler with ice packs until the last minute
